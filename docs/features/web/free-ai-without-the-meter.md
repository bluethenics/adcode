# Free AI, every day

Sign in with a verified ADCode account and choose an included model. No API key, subscription or credit card is required. All Heabsy models and Tagflow AI share one allowance of **10 credits per day**, across conversations, agents and signed-in devices.

## How the daily allowance works

The 24-hour window begins with your first included AI request. Usage resets when the window ends. Unused daily credits do not roll over. Switching included models uses the same balance; it does not create a second allowance.

AI usage and the composer meter show what is left and the refill time. A task paused at its allowance checks again automatically while ADCode remains open. Buying tokens or an active plan can let it continue sooner. Your own provider connections have their own billing and limits.

## Plans and token packs

Plans increase your daily included AI allowance. The billing interval and usage reset are separate:

- **Weekly:** $3 per week, 150 credits daily.
- **Monthly:** $9 per month, 150 credits daily.
- **Pro:** $29 per month, 600 included AI credits daily and two friend invites.

Purchased credits are used after a finite daily allowance and never expire. Top-ups are $10 for 5,000 credits, $25 for 14,000, or $50 for 30,000. A qualifying Pro referral gives each account 100 credits, once per friend. Manage billing, cancel, or redeem codes on [AI plans and tokens](https://adcode.bluethenics.com/ai-tokens).

## Availability and recovery

Provider availability, model context limits and upstream capacity still apply to every plan. More credits do not remove provider capacity limits. Temporary failures retry before any answer is delivered. If an included response is interrupted, ADCode can continue from saved progress; completed tools are retained rather than replayed. Recovery is bounded and Stop cancels it.

The relay records request outcomes, timing, token counts and fixed error categories. It does not record your prompts or replies in its accounting tables. See [Privacy](/privacy).
