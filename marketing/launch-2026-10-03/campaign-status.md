## Actual measurement

Observed in the authenticated ADCode analytics dashboard on 4 October, around 10:22 a.m. IST:

| Metric | Observed value | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Developer accounts | 641 | Total accounts, not newly acquired by this campaign |
| Active today | 10 | Editor activity in the last 24 hours; includes returning developers |
| Active this week | 108 | Last seven days |
| Active this month | 309 | Last 30 days |
| Website sessions, last 30 days | 112 | Consenting sessions; not unique people; dates use UTC |
| Sessions with install intent | 8 (7.1%) | Copied install command or download click, not a completed install |
| Installation-page sessions with install intent | 3 of 20 (15%) | Same-session install actions, not completed installation |

The dashboard defines activity as fetching an ad or reporting a day of work. It does not establish that 10 people are new, that they completed a first meaningful task, or that this campaign caused their activity. The landing page earlier showed 635 accounts; differing times and caching prevent attributing the difference to these posts.

The 200-new-user target remains unverified. Measure campaign clicks, installs, first editor use, and first task separately. Answer actual setup questions and help interested people reach a concrete result before increasing post volume.

After the first Threads batch on 4 October, the refreshed dashboard displayed 642 total accounts, 9 active in the last 24 hours, 67 active in seven days, and 296 active in 30 days. Website analytics displayed 311 page views, 114 consenting sessions, and 8 install-intent sessions (7.0%); the installation-page funnel remained 3 of 20 (15%). No oct04_* campaign rows were displayed, so no new users or installs can be attributed to this batch. The original Threads post displayed 64 views, and two authors appeared in activity against our replies. These are engagement signals only. See [Threads outreach and measurement](threads-oct04.md) for all public reply links and limitations.

At 9:37 p.m. IST on 4 October, after the expanded outreach, total accounts remained 642 and active in the last 24 hours remained 9. Website totals were 314 page views and 115 consenting sessions, with 8 install-intent sessions (7.0%). The extra three page views and one session were unattributed/direct traffic; no oct04_* campaign row appeared. Four developer accounts had replied to our questions; one codes solely on Android, and we clarified that ADCode does not run there. No new signup, completed installation or first task from Threads is verified. Six additional replies contain tracked download invitations.

Around 10:14 p.m. IST, handled incoming replies with nine more published answers across eight conversations and ten likes. Explained product economics, acknowledged skepticism, offered a tracked Loomenio task trial and setup help, responded to founder discussions and a joke, and linked official Pillow guidance for a conditional compatibility fix. One interested developer disclosed macOS; clarified that its download is unavailable. Loomenio's maker thanked us for the explanation. These are conversations and interest signals; no new signup or installation was verified. Exact text and permalinks are in threads-reply-followups-oct04.json.
