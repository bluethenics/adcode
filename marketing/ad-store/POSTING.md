If you pay to promote it, use what the last two boosts taught us:
- Run a **Website traffic** campaign with a **video website card**, not Boost. Boost bought
  impressions and almost no visits.
- Point the card at the same `utm_campaign=store-launch` link. The site sends Windows visitors
  straight to the Store installer, and phones get "Send to my computer".
- Judge it on link clicks and installs, not views. Check the 3-second view rate early;
  look at cost per click only after 30–50 clicks.
