## Messaging

The replies identify ADCode as a free/open-source Windows/Linux editor for local repo work. They explain terminal, Git and optional own-key/local AI, with separate cloud-provider charges. Where the funding model is relevant, they state that sponsored cards credit users 50% of attributed ad revenue and that earnings vary. The common first trial is open a small repo, make one change, run it and review the diff. Review invitations ask for specific setup or first-session feedback.
