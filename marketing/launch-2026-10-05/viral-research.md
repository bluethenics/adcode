# ADCode campaign research — 5 October 2026

No algorithm can guarantee virality or 200 new users. This run improves the creative and measures actual outcomes rather than claiming a guaranteed result.

## Account evidence

An existing Instagram reel opened from the most-engaged content list had 228 views, 199 viewers, 96.9% non-follower views and 8 interactions (6 likes and 2 saves). It had 0 comments, 0 shares and 0 follows. These are a baseline for that specific reel, not the fresh reel’s results. The newer pre-existing “Get paid to wait and build” reel was a different creative.

## Sources and decisions

- [Meta’s Threads educational insights](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/find-your-community-with-new-threads-educational-insights/): Meta reports that replies account for almost half of views, humor attracts more views, and media with text context and posts that create conversations tend to perform better. Applied: a short AI-waiting joke, explanatory copy and tailored replies to actual developer discussions. These are platform-level observations, not a prediction for this account.
- [Meta’s Threads topics update](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/03/new-threads-features-more-personalized-experience-you-control/): Meta reports that posts with tagged topics generally receive more views. Applied: the relevant AI Threads topic on the video post.
- [Meta’s original-content update](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/01/2026-ai-drives-performance/): Meta describes increased recommendations of original content. Applied: ADCode’s own mascot animation and original captions, without another creator’s watermark.
- [Instagram’s creator best-practices hub](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/) and [Reels watch-time tools](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/04/instagram-reels-trending-audio-and-gifts-updates/): use account-specific performance and watch time to judge results. Applied: a legible opening hook, 9:16 crop and short 29-second story. Retention and shares still need to be evaluated after distribution.
- [Instagram Trial Reels](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/): this feature can test content with non-followers. No Trial Reel control was offered in the web composer, so the publication is a normal reel; it is not labelled as a trial.
- [X recommendation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/recommendations): recommendations use multiple signals and seek relevant, meaningful conversations. Applied: an X-specific concise caption and a direct product link. No claimed formula or hidden algorithm weights.
- [Stack Overflow’s September 2026 survey retrospective](https://stackoverflow.blog/2026/09/30/getting-ready-for-2026-results-a-look-back-on-developer-survey-findings/): checked the existing animation’s 2025 survey and April 2026 pulse figures. The 31% and 59% figures come from different respondent samples; the Instagram caption makes that distinction.

## Product facts

Checked against the [live download page](https://adcode.bluethenics.com/versions) and [parallel-agent guide](https://adcode.bluethenics.com/docs/ai-parallel-agents). ADCode is a free, open-source editor for Windows and Linux. It credits users 50% of attributed ad revenue; earnings vary and eligibility rules apply. Bring your own AI key or compatible local model; cloud AI costs are separate. Parallel agents can be set from 1 to 6, with 3 as the default; extra work queues and provider limits still apply.

Illustrated workflow and example amounts are disclosed in captions. No invented testimonials, earnings, downloads or user totals were added.

## Measurement

New download/guide links have platform/campaign parameters where appropriate. Instagram uses the existing bio link. Assess each post through watch time/retention, shares, saves, replies and profile/link activity; measure actual attributed installs or activations separately. The completed work establishes publications and conversations. It does not establish new users or income.
