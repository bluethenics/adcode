# Trademarks

ADCode's source code is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). That licence covers
the code. It does not cover the ADCode name, logo or icon, which belong to Bluethenics.
Section 6 of the licence says the same thing in general terms; this page says what it means
here.

## What you can do

- Build ADCode from this repository and use it, for anything.
- Fork it, change it, and distribute your version, including commercially.
- Say truthfully that your project is "based on ADCode" or "a fork of ADCode".

## What you cannot do

- Distribute a modified build under the name ADCode, or with the ADCode logo or icon.
  Pick another name and replace the artwork in `build/`.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests Bluethenics made, endorses or supports your
  version.
- Point a renamed build at ADCode's ad server or sign-in while presenting it as ADCode.
  The service at adcode.bluethenics.com is covered by its own terms, not by the licence.

## Why

People who install something called ADCode are trusting that it shows the ads we say it
shows, records the receipts we say it records, and pays what the ledger says. That promise
only holds for builds we publish. A changed build is welcome; it needs its own name so
nobody mistakes one for the other.
